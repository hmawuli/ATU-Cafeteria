# Privacy Policy — Draft for Legal Review

This document is a drafting template, not final legal advice.

ATU Cafeteria collects account, contact, order, payment reference, device and support information needed to provide restaurant services, protect accounts, process transactions and support customers.

Customers should be informed about:
- what information is collected;
- why it is processed;
- retention periods;
- payment and service providers;
- notification/device data;
- support and fraud-prevention records;
- customer rights and account closure;
- how to contact the data controller.

Financial and order records may be retained after account closure where required for reconciliation, fraud prevention, accounting or legal obligations.

Before public launch, this document must be completed and reviewed against the applicable data-protection requirements of the operating jurisdiction.
