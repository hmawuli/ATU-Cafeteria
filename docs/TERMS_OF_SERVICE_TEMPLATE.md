# Terms of Service — Draft for Legal Review

This document is a drafting template, not final legal advice.

The final terms should define:
- account responsibilities;
- ordering and payment rules;
- menu availability and substitutions;
- cancellation windows;
- refund rules;
- pickup and fulfilment obligations;
- prohibited use;
- service availability;
- complaint/support process;
- limitation of liability;
- governing law;
- contact information.

Publish the final approved terms before production customer onboarding.
