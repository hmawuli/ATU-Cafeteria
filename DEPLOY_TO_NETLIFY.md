# 🚀 Quick 1-Minute Netlify Deployment Guide for ATU Cafeteria

Follow these simple steps to put your ATU Cafeteria web app live online on Netlify:

### Method A: Drag & Drop (Zero installation, takes 30 seconds)
1. Export / Download your project as a ZIP from the top-right menu (`⋮` or Settings in AI Studio).
2. Unzip the downloaded file on your computer.
3. Go to **[https://app.netlify.com/drop](https://app.netlify.com/drop)**.
4. Drag and drop the **`web_app`** folder into the browser circle.
5. 🎉 Netlify will immediately give you a live HTTPS web address (e.g. `https://atu-cafeteria.netlify.app`) that opens instantly on every iPhone and laptop!

### Method B: Push to GitHub & Connect Netlify
1. In AI Studio, click **Push to GitHub**.
2. Go to **[https://app.netlify.com](https://app.netlify.com)** -> Click **Add new site** -> **Import an existing project**.
3. Select your GitHub repository.
4. Netlify will automatically detect the included `netlify.toml` and deploy the site!
